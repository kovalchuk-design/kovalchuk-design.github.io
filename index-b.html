Working with AI

Designing with AI, and for AI that has to be accountable to a compliance review.

Designing with AI

  1. Base research — AI-assisted synthesis of market, competitor, and user research before a single wireframe exists
  2. IA & navigation — exploring structural options fast, then validating the one that survives scrutiny
  3. Workflow wireframes — AI-accelerated iteration, human-decided information hierarchy
  4. FSD documentation — AI-assisted drafting of the specs engineering actually reads
  5. Feature tests — AI-supported test case generation before handoff, not after bugs show up

Designing for regulated AI

  1. Confidence-scored automation — a threshold decides what ships automatically and what a human sees first
  2. Traceable sourcing — every AI-recognized value links back to its exact source, so a reviewer can verify, not just trust
  3. Audit trails — surfacing what the system decided and why, in the interface, not buried in a log file
  4. Override paths — designing the moment a human has to disagree with the model as a first-class interaction
  5. Decision documentation — making AI reasoning legible enough to survive a compliance review

AI speeds up the draft. I own what ships.
