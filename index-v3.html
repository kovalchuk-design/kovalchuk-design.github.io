AI in my process

With it, and for it

Designing with AI, and for AI that has to be accountable to a compliance review.

CH-1

Designing with AI

  • 01Base research — AI-assisted synthesis of market, competitor, and user research before a single wireframe exists
  • 02IA & navigation — exploring structural options fast, then validating the one that survives scrutiny
  • 03Workflow wireframes — AI-accelerated iteration, human-decided information hierarchy
  • 04FSD documentation — AI-assisted drafting of the specs engineering actually reads
  • 05Feature tests — AI-supported test case generation before handoff, not after bugs show up

CH-2

Designing for regulated AI

  • 01Confidence-scored automation — a threshold decides what ships automatically and what a human sees first
  • 02Traceable sourcing — every AI-recognized value links back to its exact source, so a reviewer can verify, not just trust
  • 03Audit trails — surfacing what the system decided and why, in the interface, not buried in a log file
  • 04Override paths — designing the moment a human has to disagree with the model as a first-class interaction
  • 05Decision documentation — making AI reasoning legible enough to survive a compliance review

AI speeds up the draft. I own what ships.
